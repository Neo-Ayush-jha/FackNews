How it works

Model + Newsroom language cues

This application uses a BERT classifier trained on the Kaggle Fake and True news dataset. It evaluates language patterns to predict whether an article reads like verified reporting or misinformation.

Signal features

Headlines, tone, and source patterns are encoded by the transformer layers.

Decision layer

A lightweight classifier head converts embeddings into a binary prediction.

Training data

Kaggle Fake.csv and True.csv with labeled headlines and text.

Inference

Text is tokenized, padded, and fed to the fine-tuned BERT model.

Confidence

Scores show the model's probability for the predicted class.

Tips for best results

  • Paste full paragraphs, not just a headline.
  • Include the original article body when possible.
  • Use the URL option to auto-extract text.
  • Compare multiple sources for verification.

Responsible use

Predictions are probabilistic. For high impact stories, corroborate with multiple credible sources and verify the publisher before sharing.
