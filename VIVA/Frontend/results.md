# results.html Viva Questions

## 1. results page ka purpose kya hai?
Answer: Detailed final prediction dikhana.

## 2. result section me kya show hota hai?
Answer: Final label, confidence, aur confidence gauge.

## 3. history section kya dikhata hai?
Answer: Recent checked news items.

## 4. clear saved result button kya karta hai?
Answer: Stored latest prediction remove karta hai.

## 5. signal score ka role kya hai?
Answer: Suspicious language level show karna.

## 6. primary_prediction ka kya meaning hai?
Answer: Main detector ka output.

## 7. primary_model_status kyun dikhaya jata hai?
Answer: Model ready hai ya warming mode me tha, ye batane ke liye.

## 8. confidence breakdown me kya hota hai?
Answer: Fake likelihood, real likelihood, aur final confidence.

## 9. history cards ka use kya hai?
Answer: Previous predictions ko quickly reopen karne ke liye.

## 10. article insights section me kya counts milte hain?
Answer: Words, sentences, characters, aur signal score.
